Geography codes

PortugalINE.Codes — Module

Geography codes as Statistics Portugal issues them.

Every INE indicator that varies by place carries a code in dimension 2 (dimension 1 is the reference period). The codes form a prefix hierarchy, and their lengths are the only thing that says which level a row describes:

lengthlevelexample
2country, or NUTS IIPT, 11 (Norte)
1NUTS I1 (Continente)
3NUTS III111 (Alto Minho), 11D (Douro)
7municipality1111601 (Arcos de Valdevez), 11D1819 (Tabuaço)
9parish (freguesia)11C130741 (Vila Boa de Quires e Maureles)
4municipality, bare DICO1307 (Census 2021 indicators)
6parish, bare DICOFRE130741 (Census 2021 indicators)

INE uses two forms. Most indicators prefix the NUTS III code (7 and 9 characters). The Census 2021 indicators give the bare official code instead: the 4-digit DICO for a municipality and the 6-digit DICOFRE for a parish. Measured on 2026-11-25: indicators 0012328 and 0012273 (unemployment and illiteracy, Census 2021) carry 308 four-character and 3,092 six-character codes and no seven- or nine-character ones. Before the bare forms were recognised, municipal_values found no municipal rows in them and said the indicator stopped at NUTS III, which was false.

A municipality code is not seven digits. Its first three characters are the NUTS III code, which may contain a letter: 14 of the 26 NUTS III codes do, among them 11A to 11E, 1A0, 1B0 and 1C1 to 1D3. Matching municipalities with a seven-digit pattern such as r"[0-9]{7}" keeps 147 of the 308 and drops the rest without complaint, which is the mistake this module exists to make impossible.

The last four characters are the DICO, the official municipality code (district and municipality within it). It is numeric for all 308 municipalities and unique across them, so it is the key to join INE indicators to a geometry. The NUTS III prefix is not: NUTS III regions and districts cross-cut each other. Arouca is 11A0104, a municipality of Aveiro district (DICO 01 + 04) in the Porto metropolitan NUTS III region, and nothing in the first three characters recovers its district.

PortugalINE.Codes.dico — Method
dico(code) -> String

The DICO of a municipality code: its last four characters, the official district-and-municipality number. This is the key to join INE indicators to a geometry, because it is numeric and unique across all 308 while the NUTS III prefix is neither a district nor unique to one.

julia> dico("11A0104")   # Arouca
"0104"

PortugalINE.Codes.dicofre — Method
dicofre(code) -> String

The DICOFRE of a parish code: its last six characters, the official district, municipality and parish number. It is the parish key, as the DICO is the municipal one, and CAOP's dtmnfr property carries the same code. Its first four digits are the parish's DICO.

A DICOFRE is not always numeric. Its last two characters are digits for every parish except the eight 2013 unions of Barcelos (DICO 0302), coded 0302FA to 0302FH because the municipality had more than 99 parishes. Checking for six digits would drop those eight. That was the first version of this function, and the real data stopped it.

julia> dicofre("11C130741")
"130741"

julia> dicofre("130741")   # the bare form of the Census 2021 indicators
"130741"

PortugalINE.Codes.geolevel — Method
geolevel(code) -> GeoLevel

The level code describes, from its length. Throws ArgumentError on a length INE does not use, rather than guessing: an unexpected length means the code came from somewhere this parser does not model, and silently treating it as the nearest level is how a whole geographic tier goes missing from an analysis.

julia> geolevel("1111601")
MUNICIPALITY::GeoLevel = 4

julia> geolevel("11D")
NUTS3::GeoLevel = 3

PortugalINE.Codes.ismainland — Method
ismainland(code) -> Bool
isisland(code) -> Bool

Whether a municipality or parish is on the mainland or in the autonomous regions, by district number. This distinction is not cosmetic: the 30 island municipalities share no land border with anything, so in a contiguity weights matrix over all 308 they are isolated vertices whose rows are entirely zero. Row-standardising such a matrix divides by zero. Decide between the 278 mainland municipalities and a distance- or neighbour-count-based weighting before building W, rather than discovering it in a singular matrix.

PortugalINE.Codes.ispseudo — Method
ispseudo(code) -> Bool

Whether code is one of the rows INE adds that are not places. Live births (0008234) carries, at every level of the hierarchy: residence abroad ("Estrangeiro", "Foreigner") as 0, 00, 000, 0000000, 000000000 and YY; unknown residence ("Ignorado", "Unknown") as 9, 99, 999, 9999999, 999999999; and the grand total as T. Their lengths are those of real levels, so without this check geolevel would call T a NUTS I region and 000000000 a parish – and ismainland took the abroad row for a mainland parish. geolevel refuses them, and ismunicipality and isparish reject them, so municipal_values and parish_values leave them out.
